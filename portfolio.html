NMSU Study Room Rating App

A "Rate My Professors"-style app for finding study spaces on the NMSU campus. Students can browse existing study rooms or add a new location, then leave their own reviews to help others pick the right spot.

  • Create a new study room location or find an existing one
  • Write and read reviews for each location
  • Filter locations by features such as food, computers, and other amenities
  • Search for rooms by name
  • Interactive map of study locations
